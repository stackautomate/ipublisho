About iPublisho

Small books can hold big things.

We are an independent publishing company making useful, joyful, and emotionally intelligent books for real life.

Our point of view

Publishing is an act of attention.

iPublisho exists to make books that people want to give, keep, and use. We look for the useful idea, the honest question, or the playful challenge hiding in plain sight, then give it a form that feels welcoming.

Our list is growing across children, puzzles, preschool, and teen publishing. The subjects may change, but the standard stays the same: thoughtful words, clear structure, and design with a human pulse.

We work with writers, illustrators, educators, and makers who believe a book can be both beautifully considered and genuinely helpful.
