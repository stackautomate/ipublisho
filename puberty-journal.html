A shared space for honest conversations

Growing up brings questions, feelings, surprises, and moments that stay with us forever.

Growing Up Together is a guided puberty journal made for girls and the women who care about them. Inside are 100 heartfelt prompts that invite honest conversations about body changes, first periods, moods, confidence, and the experience of growing up.

This book is designed to be shared with a mother, elder sister, aunt, cousin, best friend, or any trusted woman who wants to listen, respond, and connect. Some pages may bring laughter. Some may bring memories. Some may open the door to the kind of conversation many girls wish they had earlier.

Whether used as a personal journal, a keepsake gift, or a shared reflection book, this is a gentle space to write openly and feel understood.
