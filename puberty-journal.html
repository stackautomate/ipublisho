A shared space for honest conversations

Growing up can bring big questions and changes that are hard to put into words. This journal gives girls and the women who care about them a warm, welcoming place to begin.

Puberty Journal: Share Your Growing Up Story is an essential keepsake guide for mothers, aunts, and mentors to pass along their own stories, listen without judgment, and make the conversations around growing up feel a little less awkward.

Its 100 heartfelt prompts open up thoughtful conversations about physical transitions, mood swings, self-esteem, and building confidence. Each question offers a gentle starting point for sharing memories, making sense of change, and reminding a young person she is not alone.

Write together, take turns, or let the journal become a personal reflection. However you use it, these pages make room for honesty, connection, and a keepsake to return to.
