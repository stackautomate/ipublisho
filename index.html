Why this journal matters

Growing up can feel very lonely.

As a young girl watches her body change, she faces confusion and big emotional shifts. She may have deep questions but not know how to ask them. The Puberty Journal for Girls offers a safe way to start, with space for trusted mentors to share real stories, build confidence, and remind her she is not alone.
