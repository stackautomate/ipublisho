About iPublisho

Small books can hold big things.

We are an independent publishing company making useful, joyful, and emotionally intelligent books for real life.

Our mission

Publishing is an act of attention.

iPublisho makes thoughtful, useful books that people want to give, keep, and return to. We look for the honest question, memorable story, or practical idea that can make everyday life a little richer, then shape it into a book with care.

Our publishing brings together journals, fiction, and books for teens. Across every subject, we value clear writing, considered design, and ideas that meet readers with warmth and respect.

We work with writers, illustrators, educators, and makers who believe books can be both beautifully considered and genuinely helpful.
