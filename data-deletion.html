Want us to remove your data?

We will help you understand what we hold and remove it where we are able to.

How to request deletion

Email info@ipublisho.com with “Data deletion request” in the subject line. Please tell us the email address or details associated with your previous enquiry so we can identify the right information.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your request and take reasonable steps to delete personal information that we hold, unless we need to retain it for a legal or legitimate business reason. We may ask for enough information to verify the request and protect against unauthorised deletion.

For questions about privacy, please contact info@ipublisho.com.
